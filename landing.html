pt-PT ≠ pt-BR

Vocabulary, verb conjugation, and register genuinely diverge. Models trained on pt-BR data reliably produce phrasing that reads as wrong to a Portugal-based audience — every row here is checked against it. AMALIA proves the point at a national scale — Portugal's own open-source LLM was built specifically because generic and Brazilian-Portuguese models weren't good enough for pt-PT; this dataset takes that same principle into the customer-support domain.
